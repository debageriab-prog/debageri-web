# Branding

## Identity

**Debageri AB** is a Swedish IT consultancy that presents itself as approachable, technically excellent, and quietly premium. Not corporate. Not generic SaaS.

**Personality:** Imagine if Apple, Linear, and a Swedish coffee shop designed an engineering consultancy.

**Keywords:** Scandinavian · Premium · Minimal · Warm · Technical · Trustworthy · Friendly

## Name and logo

The name *Debageri* is a portmanteau of:
- **debug** — what engineers do
- **bageri** — Swedish for *bakery*
- **Bagheri** — the founder's surname

The logo mark is a stylised bug (insect) whose body subtly references a circuit board. It should feel crafted and intentional, not clipart.

## Typography

- **Font:** Inter (Google Fonts, variable) — modern, legible, friendly
- **Headings:** large, semibold (600), tight letter-spacing, generous line height
- **Body:** base size (16px), relaxed line height (1.6–1.75), medium brown
- **Labels/eyebrows:** tiny (11–12px), widely spaced, uppercase, tan colour
- **Code/technical:** system monospace

## Voice and tone

- Direct and confident — no corporate waffle
- Warm but not informal
- Technical precision without jargon for its own sake
- Swedish restraint: say less, mean more

## Visual language

- **Whitespace:** generous — breathing room is a design feature
- **Corners:** rounded (`rounded-2xl`, `rounded-full` for pills and buttons)
- **Borders:** very subtle — `border-[#e8d8c8]` on cards and dividers
- **Bakery/debug details:** circuit traces, bug iconography, code snippets as decoration — always subtle, never gimmicky
- **Photography:** real photos of people and workspaces; no stock photography
- **Illustrations:** sparse, geometric, SVG line art in brand colours

## Avoid

- Bright gradients
- Generic SaaS blues/purples
- Dark mode (the site uses a warm beige — dark mode would require careful palette work)
- Stock photographs
- Excessive micro-animations
- Cluttered layouts
